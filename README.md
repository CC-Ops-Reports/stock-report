## NVIDIA CORPORATION (NVDA) ⭐

**Discussion Driver:** NVIDIA reported its largest quarterly revenue ever at $81.62B (+85% YoY) for Q1 FY2027, crushing all estimates, with $75.2B in data center revenue alone. The company announced an $80 billion share buyback and raised its dividend, yet shares declined modestly as investors 'sold the news' following the stock's run to near all-time highs. Despite the pullback, the results validated the entire AI infrastructure investment thesis and sent chip sector peers sharply higher.

**Bull Case:**
- Blowout Q1 FY2027 results ($81.62B revenue, +85% YoY) with guidance pointing to sustained $100B+ quarterly revenue solidify NVDA as the dominant AI infrastructure pick and validate the ongoing supercycle
- $80B share buyback and dividend increase signal management's confidence in sustained free cash flow; forward P/E of ~26x is far more reasonable than trailing given rapid earnings growth trajectory

**Bear Case:**
- 'Sell the news' dynamic post-earnings — stock declined despite a record beat, suggesting expectations were already fully priced in after the prior run to all-time highs near $235
- China export restrictions on H20 and advanced chips block a major addressable market and remove a meaningful near-term growth driver from one of NVDA's largest markets

**Recommendation:** Buy
