## SNOWFLAKE INC. (SNOW) ⭐

**Discussion Driver:** Snowflake surged 37% on May 28 after reporting blowout Q1 FY2027 earnings ($0.39 EPS vs. $0.32 estimated; $1.39B revenue, +33% YoY) alongside a $6B multi-year AWS partnership to accelerate global enterprise AI adoption. Product revenue grew 34% YoY — what CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy called an AI inflection point — and at least 25 analysts raised price targets, pushing the post-earnings median consensus to $275 from $230 pre-earnings.

**Bull Case:**
- $6B AWS spending commitment secures long-term revenue visibility and positions Snowflake as the enterprise AI data layer, with access to Graviton processors and AWS AI infrastructure deepening platform integration
- Product revenue accelerating to 34% YoY growth as enterprises deploy AI/ML workloads on Snowflake; raised FY2027 product revenue guidance to $5.84B (+31%) with operating margin guidance lifted to 13.5%

**Bear Case:**
- $6B AWS commitment is a major cost outflow (~$1.2B/year) that will pressure near-term free cash flow and extend the timeline to GAAP profitability, with the company still reporting negative GAAP EPS
- Intense competition from Databricks, Google BigQuery, and Microsoft Fabric threatens market share in the rapidly evolving data cloud sector where hyperscalers are offering increasingly capable native alternatives

**Recommendation:** Buy

- Best comparable sales performance in four years suggests the turnaround strategy is gaining traction, with the Sephora partnership and private-label refresh driving incremental traffic and basket size improvements
- Stock trades at just ~6x trailing earnings near multi-year lows — significant upside potential exists if recovery momentum builds into the back-to-school and holiday seasons

**Bear Case:**
- Analyst consensus remains Sell — department store retail faces persistent structural headwinds from e-commerce disruption and off-price competition (TJX, Burlington) that one quarter of improving comps cannot reverse
