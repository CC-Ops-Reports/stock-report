## NETFLIX (NFLX) ⭐

**Discussion Driver:** Netflix Q2 2026 revenue of $12.56B missed the $12.6B consensus and Q3 guidance of $12.86B fell short of the $13B estimate, triggering a ~7% post-earnings selloff.
