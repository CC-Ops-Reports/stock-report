## ROKU INC. (ROKU) ⭐

**Discussion Driver:** Fox Corporation announced today (June 15) a definitive agreement to acquire Roku for $160.00 per share in a combination of cash ($96.00/share) and FOX Class A stock (0.9693 shares per Roku share), valuing Roku at ~$22B in enterprise value. The deal confirmed long-running takeover speculation that had sent Roku up 20% on June 12 on analyst upgrades and index addition. With the official deal announcement, Roku shares now trade as a merger arbitrage play at a ~12.6% discount to the $160 deal price, as regulatory approval and closing is expected in H1 2027.

**Bull Case:**
- The Fox $160/share acquisition offer provides a hard floor and confirmed upside of ~12.6% from current prices if the deal closes as expected; both boards unanimously approved the transaction.
- Roku's 100M+ global connected TV households and first-party audience data make it the largest independent streaming gateway in the U.S., combining with Fox's live sports, Fox News, and Tubi to create a powerful live+streaming platform.

**Bear Case:**
- Jefferies and William Blair downgraded Roku to Hold post-announcement; the deal closes in H1 2027, leaving shares exposed to 12+ months of regulatory risk, deal-break risk, and stock-consideration value fluctuations tied to FOXA performance.
- The $160 acquisition price caps meaningful upside from current levels; if the deal is blocked or falls through, ROKU could retrace sharply toward pre-announcement levels (~$119-$120).

**Recommendation:** Hold

## FOX CORPORATION (FOXA)

**Discussion Driver:** Fox Corporation shocked markets today by announcing a $22B acquisition of Roku at $160/share in a cash-and-stock deal. FOXA shares plunged ~17% on the news, with the deal reference price set at $66.03 (10-day VWAP as of June 10). Investors punished Fox for what they perceive as an expensive acquisition that significantly dilutes shareholders (adding ~30M+ FOX Class A shares) and stretches the balance sheet. The stock's reaction represents one of the worst single-day M&A-driven sell-offs for a major U.S. media company in recent memory.

**Bull Case:**
- The Fox-Roku combination creates a compelling live TV + streaming platform with 100M connected TV households, giving Fox a dominant connected TV distribution channel for Fox News, Fox Sports, and Tubi — arguably the most valuable streaming combination after Netflix and Disney+.
- Morgan Stanley maintained a $70 price target post-deal, implying over 28% upside from today's depressed close; long-term strategic synergies from unified ad-tech, first-party data, and live sports monetization could justify the premium paid.

**Bear Case:**
- The market's -17% single-day reaction signals decisive investor disapproval; at 0.9693 FOX Class A shares per Roku share, Fox is issuing significant equity at a discount to the target's valuation, potentially permanently diluting existing Fox shareholders.
- The deal does not close until H1 2027, creating over 12 months of M&A overhang, regulatory scrutiny from DOJ/FTC, and balance sheet uncertainty; any weakening of FOXA stock could erode the stock-consideration value and complicate the deal.

**Recommendation:** Hold
