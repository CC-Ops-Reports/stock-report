## NUVALENT, INC. (NUVL)

**Discussion Driver:** GSK announced a $10.6B acquisition of Nuvalent at $124/share — a 40% premium to the prior close of $88.49 — marking the industry's second-largest deal of 2026. The stock surged ~39% to $123.19 on June 9, eclipsing its prior 52-week high of $113.02. The deal targets Nuvalent's late-stage ROS1 and ALK inhibitors (zidesamtinib, neladalkib) for NSCLC; the FDA separately granted Priority Review to neladalkib with a PDUFA date of November 27, 2026.

**Bull Case:**
- GSK acquisition at $124/share provides near-certain risk-arbitrage return with only ~0.7% gap to close; deal is backed by GSK's balance sheet targeting transformative oncology assets with FDA-validated pipeline
- FDA Priority Review for neladalkib as a potential best-in-class next-generation ALK inhibitor confirms pipeline quality and strengthens the strategic rationale behind the $10.6B price tag

**Bear Case:**
- Deal-break risk: regulatory hurdles, antitrust scrutiny, or shareholder litigation could collapse the acquisition and send shares back toward the pre-announcement $88 range — nearly 29% downside
- With stock at $123.19 vs. the $124 deal price, risk/reward is highly asymmetric for new buyers; only 0.7% upside versus ~29% downside if the deal falls through

**Recommendation:** Hold
